How many players?
  1. Mash up a hero: pick a head, a body and legs from different characters — just like pulling apart Mix-Up Mashers!
  2. Take turns spinning the wheel. The hero you land on tells you how far to move.
  3. Watch out for Loki, Hulk Buster, Ultron and Red Skull spaces!
  4. First Masher to the Finish wins!
